Tax Preparation Document Checklist
Gather these documents before your appointment. Don't worry if you're missing something — we'll guide you through it.

Basic Information

  • Full legal name
  • Date of birth
  • Current address
  • Social Insurance Number
  • Phone number and email address

Family Information (if applicable)

  • Spouse's name, SIN, and date of birth
  • Names and dates of birth of any dependents

Income Slips

  • T4 – Employment income
  • T4A – Pension, contract, or other income
  • T5 – Investment income
  • T3 – Trust income
  • T4RSP or T4RIF
  • T5008 – Investment sales
  • Any other tax slips received

Additional Income (if applicable)

  • Self-employment or contract income
  • Rental income
  • Investment or cryptocurrency trading
  • Foreign income

Deductions and Credits (if applicable)

  • RRSP contribution receipts
  • Tuition slips (T2202)
  • Medical expenses
  • Charitable donation receipts
  • Childcare expense receipts
  • Union or professional dues
  • Moving expenses
  • Home office expenses (if self-employed)

Other Information

  • Your most recent CRA Notice of Assessment
  • Direct deposit information with CRA (if not already set up)
  • Details of any major life changes during the year (marriage, separation, new child, moving provinces, etc.)
